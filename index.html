What is the Tip Calculator?

The Tip Calculator works out the right gratuity and the total to pay, and splits the bill evenly between any number of people. Enter the bill, choose a tip percentage, and get each person's share in seconds — perfect for restaurants, bars, and group meals.

How it works

The tip is tip = bill × (tipPercent ÷ 100), the total is bill + tip, and each person pays total ÷ numberOfPeople. Choosing a higher percentage or a smaller group raises the per-person amount, while a larger group lowers it.

Example 1: A $80 bill at 18% adds a $14.40 tip for a $94.40 total; split between 4 people that is $23.60 each.
Example 2: A $52.50 bill at 20% adds a $10.50 tip for a $63.00 total.

When to use it

Quickly figure out a fair tip at a restaurant or café. Split the check evenly with friends without awkward mental math. Work out gratuity for a taxi, delivery, or salon. Or double-check a bill that already added a service charge.

FAQ

How much should I tip?

In the US, 15–20% of the pre-tax bill is customary for table service, with 20% common for good service. Norms vary by country and situation.

Can I split between multiple people?

Yes. Enter the number of people and the calculator divides the total (bill plus tip) evenly so everyone pays a fair share.

Is the Tip Calculator free?

Yes, it is free, requires no sign-up, and runs in your browser.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. It is designed for quick use on a phone at the table.

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

Tipping on the pre-tax subtotal is standard, though many people simply tip on the total for convenience — the difference is usually small.
